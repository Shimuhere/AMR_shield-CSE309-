How to use the system

Follow these simple steps based on your role.

For Pharmacies

  • 1. Log in to your pharmacy dashboard.
  • 2. Record sales as they happen or connect your own software.
  • 3. All data is sent safely to the central system.

For Patients

  • 1. Create a free account and set your location on the map.
  • 2. Keep a history of the medicines you take.
  • 3. Check for health alerts in your area anytime.

For Health Officials

  • 1. Monitor the nationwide risk map.
  • 2. Change usage limits to trigger alerts.
  • 3. Download full reports for better health planning.
